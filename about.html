ABOUT US

FIRE EAGLE SECURITY & SAFETY SYSTEMS is one of the leading suppliers of Safety and Fire Fighting Equipment’s and systems.
It also provides the Installation of fire protection system, Annual maintenance and repair of fire protection systems.

FIRE EAGLE SECURITY & SAFETY SYSTEMS was established in 2010 in Abu Dhabi. Since its inception, the company has endeavored to offer the best quality in the construction fields and design field therefore has been recognized as a quality conscious organization adopting the latest technologies in “Fire & Safety” engineering field including administration and project management.
